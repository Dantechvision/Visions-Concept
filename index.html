About

The person behind the lens.

Daniel Adesanya (Vision) is a Lagos-based freelancer and the founder of Visions Concept, a creative media specialist focused on using creative visual elements to communicate brand identity, brand image, clarity, character, and visual distinction.

His work spans portrait, studio, event and editorial photography, event and brand videography, and graphic design — creating visual experiences that take you beyond your screen and submerge you into a world of communicative creativity, from the first frame to the final export.

With a background in front-end web development, Daniel also brings a digital perspective to his creative work, particularly on projects where visual identity and digital experience need to work seamlessly together.

Through Visions Concept, he works with individuals, businesses, and brands looking to be seen clearly, communicate intentionally, and stand apart visually.
